The simple, beautiful transit app

For those who know where they're going.

Dot Sign is a subway countdown for your iPhone Home Screen and your Mac's menu bar. It finds the nearest station and shows the next trains, lettered like the sign on the platform.

Right where you look

A sign on your Home Screen and another on your desktop. There's no app to open.

Running in three cities

Dot Sign switches to the city you're in. Search still finds every station, anywhere.

New York

Every subway line, plus PATH, from the MTA’s realtime feeds.

Chicago

All eight ‘L’ lines, from CTA Train Tracker.

Los Angeles

Metro’s A, B, C, D, E and K Lines, from Metro’s own realtime feed.

Three sizes, three jobs

Each widget size has its own layout, so nothing is just shrunk to fit.

Small

The next train in big numbers, and the two after it.

Medium

Your station and the trains leaving it.

Large

The whole station board, both directions.

Two ways to light it

Pick a style once in Edit Widget. Every letter lands on the grid either way.

Full Color

Route colors on a dark board, with crisp white lettering.

Classic LED

One color, like the old platform clocks. Amber, red or green.

On your Mac, too

The next train sits in the menu bar, in the same dot lettering. Click it for the full sign and nearby stations. The same widgets work on the desktop and in Notification Center.

Set it up once

  1. Add the widget

    Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Dot Sign.

  2. Let it find you

    Allow location while using the app. The sign shows your nearest station and changes as you move.

  3. Glance

    That's all. If you always leave from the same place, pin that station instead.

Try it yourself

Pick a station, a size and a style, the way you would in Edit Widget. Times here are made up; the app shows real ones.
